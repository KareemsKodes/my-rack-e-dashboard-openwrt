Addressing & VLANs

Configure local networks, gateway addresses, DHCP pools, and access ports.

Help

Primary LAN is the management network for this MX. VLANs create additional local networks and can place selected LAN ports into those networks as access ports. Keep at least one LAN port on Primary LAN so you can still reach the manager.

NetworkStatusGatewayDHCP poolPorts

Enter a subnet like 10.10.10.0/24 or a gateway like 10.10.10.1. My-Rack-E uses the first usable address as the gateway, reserves space for fixed infrastructure, and builds a client DHCP range from the subnet.
